## 4. Final design summary

The approved design specifies MySQL 8.4 LTS, Flyway migration ownership, `BIGINT AUTO_INCREMENT` identifiers, server-managed session authentication, no token/JWT persistence, and no BLOB binary storage. It preserves the fixed Đại đội > Trung đội > Tiểu đội hierarchy, historical assignment and competition-attribution rules, immutable Quiz and Weekly answer semantics, Competition policy/history/integrity rules, aggregate popular-content metrics, EDU reuse of Quiz without completion tables, and atomic SHA-256 invitation registration.

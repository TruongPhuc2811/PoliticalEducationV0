## 3. Quiz Answer Historical FK

- `quiz_attempt_answers.selected_attempt_option_id` is `BIGINT NULL`, where NULL exclusively represents unanswered.
- Its dictionary and FK catalog both specify `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.
- Historical grading text and delete/update scenarios now state that a non-NULL selection cannot be erased from its immutable attempt-option snapshot.
- `quiz_attempt_question_options.source_option_id` remains `BIGINT NULL` with `ON DELETE SET NULL`, explicitly limited to live-bank traceability.

## 4. Weekly Content Freeze

No Weekly snapshot table was added.

- Before `period_opens_at`, Admin may edit question text, options (including set/text/order/correctness), explanation, and non-overlapping boundaries.
- At/after opening, question content, option rows/content/order/correctness, and explanation are immutable; option insert/delete is prohibited.
- If a submission exists, the period boundaries cannot shift.
- After close, business content remains immutable.
- The lifecycle policy, Weekly table notes, constraint catalog, query/update scenarios, and concurrency review all document the application-enforced boundary and its historical-selection purpose.
- V0.1/V0.2 do not require Admin editing while a Weekly Question is OPEN, so no business conflict was found.

## 5. Competition Criterion Resolution

- Added `uq_comp_criteria_policy_source_type` UNIQUE `(policy_id, source_type)`.
- Each policy version has at most one criterion for each approved source class: `QUIZ_RESULT`, `WEEKLY_RESULT`, and `MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT`.
- The dictionary, Business Constraint Catalog, Index Catalog, and query scenario define deterministic lookup by `(period.policy_id, source_type)`.
- No per-Quiz/per-Weekly selector, tag, formula builder, or source type was invented.

## 6. Competition Cross-Row Consistency

Documented as application-enforced relational invariants:

- Quiz contribution period/account match the canonical Quiz source selection; the selection still validates selected-result ownership and Quiz identity.
- Weekly contribution account matches submission account; `submitted_at` must fall in the applicable Competition period under the approved source-timing rule.
- Manual contribution period/account match the manual adjustment.
- Contribution criterion policy equals the period policy, and criterion `source_type` equals contribution `source_type`.

The catalog, query scenario, and concurrency review now explicitly reject cross-user, cross-period, and cross-policy contribution rows.
